# STACKER video

Style guide: `stacker-kit/README.md` (read §1 before writing). Renderer: `./studio stacker <scenario.json> [--preview t1,t2]`.

## Steps
1. **Inputs.** Ask only for what is missing: the recording (voice or video of the author), the topic/thesis, band colour, series counter.
   No recording yet → make a demo with `script` (simulated timing) and say clearly that timing will change with the real voice.
2. **Find the words.** If there is a recording, run a first preview: the renderer transcribes and caches word timings next to the
   scenario (`<name>.words.json`). Read that file — the intro pages must use the words exactly in spoken order.
3. **Write the intro (3–5 pages, 6–12 s).**
   - Stack pages: 2–3 rows of 1–3 words; break rows at natural phrase boundaries.
   - One punch page with a single contrast word (but / done / how).
   - Last page: punch + sticker + `tear` (the final word torn around the sticker).
4. **Pick stickers** from `stacker-kit/stickers/CATALOG.md`: the sticker must be the *literal* object of the key word of that page.
   No literal match → say so and propose generating one (`stacker-kit/tools/grid_prompts.json` + `make_stickers.py`). Never pick a decorative sticker.
5. **Preview.** `./studio stacker x.json --preview` at: each page start + 0.3 s, each sticker time, the tear, and 2 s after the cut.
   Look at the frames:
   - text not hidden entirely by a sticker;
   - rows don't run off the band;
   - the punch word fits;
   - captions are 2–4 words.

   Fix the scenario and repeat.
6. **Render** `./studio stacker x.json` and report: duration, where the intro ends, warnings (misheard words), and what you could not check.

## Honesty rules
- You do not hear audio. The sound design is generated by the renderer; say that you checked timing by numbers, not by ear.
- Word timings come from ASR; a warning "не найдено в речи" means a substituted time — tell the user which word.
- Don't put other people's footage or music into the video. Stickers from the kit are CC BY: remind the user about the credit line.
